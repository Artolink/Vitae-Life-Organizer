What the app is

Vitae is an organiser you keep on your phone. It holds activities, habits, goals and records across the areas of life you switch on, and it does all of that on the device itself.

There is no account, no sign-in and no server. Nothing you write is sent anywhere.

Your data, and the one consequence

Everything you enter stays in the app's own storage on your phone. It is yours, it is not read by anybody else, and it is not shared with the advertiser. The privacy policy says the same thing in the detail a store asks for.

The consequence worth stating plainly: because no copy exists anywhere else, deleting the app deletes your data, and nobody can get it back for you. The app can write a backup file whenever you ask it to, and that file is yours to keep.

Adverts and the purchase

The app shows occasional adverts through Google AdMob. One in-app purchase removes them permanently.

The purchase is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play, not by this app: payment, receipts and refunds are between you and the store, under the store's own rules. The app is told one thing, that the purchase exists, and then stops showing adverts.

If you reinstall or change phone, the same screen offers to restore it.

What the app is not

Vitae is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. It records what you tell it and does arithmetic on it. Figures such as the calorie estimate come from published formulas, which are averages and were never about you; the app says where each one comes from, on its own page inside it.

Nothing in it is financial, legal or professional advice either. Decisions about your health or your money are yours, and worth taking with somebody qualified.
